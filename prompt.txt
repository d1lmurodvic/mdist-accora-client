Accora is a financial management platform for small and mid-sized businesses.

Its main focus is automating invoice and receipt processing.

A user can take a photo of an invoice or receipt, or upload one. Accora reads the document, extracts important information like the vendor, date, amount, tax, currency, invoice number, and line items, then suggests a category. The user reviews the information and approves it. After approval, Accora saves it as a transaction and updates the company’s financial data.

Accora also includes:

Transactions — track income and expenses.

Invoices — create and manage customer invoices.

Reports — view profit and loss, cash flow, and expense breakdowns.

Forecast — see expected income, expenses, and future cash balance.

Accountant access — a simple way for businesses and accountants to work with the same financial information.

The main idea is simple:

Take a photo of a financial document → Accora understands it → turns it into structured financial data → helps the business understand its finances.

Accora is not meant to feel like an AI chatbot. AI works quietly inside the product, mainly to reduce manual accounting work.
